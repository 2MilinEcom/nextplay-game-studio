HOW IT WORKS
  1. Show the full arena

    Use an unobstructed window. Small or cropped details may stay unknown.

  2. Let a batch finish

    New feedback appears after screenshots are analyzed and checked. Capture pauses during processing.

  3. Review the evidence

    Every observation points to its actual screenshot. Possible explanations remain questions.
